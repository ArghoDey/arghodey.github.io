DTP-HMamba: Dynamic Token-Pruned Hierarchical Mamba Fusion for Incomplete-Modality Brain Tumor Segmentation

Swachha Ray, Yunfei Yin, Argho Dey, Zheng Yuan and Hongyu Liu

Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, under review.

Abstract

Complete multimodal MRI is not always available in practice, because of cost, scan time, patient condition, imaging artefacts, protocol differences or contraindications to contrast agents, and many incomplete-modality methods additionally operate on dense 3D feature representations that waste computation on background and normal tissue. We propose DTP-HMamba, a dynamic token-pruned hierarchical Mamba fusion framework. A tumour-aware token scoring mechanism selects informative tumour-related tokens before multimodal fusion; the selected tokens are integrated through a token-pruned interleaved Mamba fusion block that enables cross-modality interaction under arbitrary missing-modality conditions; and a Scale-Mamba module models dependencies across hierarchical feature levels, allowing boundary-level information and high-level semantics to interact adaptively. On BraTS 2023, DTP-HMamba obtains Dice scores of 91.0%, 86.4% and 75.5% for whole tumour, tumour core and enhancing tumour, with consistent gains in segmentation performance and boundary precision.
