RCT-AD: A Reliable Context-Aware and Temporal Planning Framework for Autonomous Driving

Argho Dey, Yunfei Yin, Swachha Ray, Md Minhazul Islam, Zheng Yuan, Sijing Xiong, Hongyu Liu and Zhiqiu Huang

Knowledge-Based Systems, under review.

Preprint

Abstract

We present RCT-AD, a semantically guided and memory-aware BEV framework that unifies perception, temporal reasoning and planning in a single representation, rather than treating each stage in isolation. A Reliable Context Awareness mechanism combines short-term sensory and long-term instance banks to preserve high-quality BEV features and filter noisy inputs; a temporal trajectory planner uses recurrent attention, combining LSTM and graph networks, to model long-horizon dependencies and interaction; and a detection and segmentation head integrates semantic supervision to strengthen contextual understanding. Through unified multi-task optimisation over segmentation, detection, prediction, planning and mapping, RCT-AD improves temporal consistency, semantic interpretability and trajectory stability. On nuScenes it reaches 52.3 mIoU (R101, 512x1408) and 0.54 m average L2 displacement with a 0.06% average collision rate, surpassing end-to-end baselines. A preprint is available on arXiv.
