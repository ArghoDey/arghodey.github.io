Enhanced BEV Scene Segmentation: De-Noise Channel Attention for Resource-Constrained Environments

Argho Dey, Yunfei Yin, Zheng Yuan, Zhiwen Zeng, Xianjian Bao and Md Minhazul Islam

Computers, Materials & Continua, 2025.

PaperCode

Abstract

Bird's-Eye-View segmentation fuses camera and radar into a single scene representation, but heavy attention blocks make it hard to run where GPU memory is scarce. We introduce ECABEV, which replaces BEVCar's deformable attention with a de-noise enhanced channel attention mechanism, a scalable cross-entropy loss and a bilinear-interpolation layer-normalisation (BLN) design. De-noise channel attention combines global average and max pooling to emphasise informative channels while filtering noisy ones, improving feature purity and cross-modal consistency; the scalable loss dynamically prioritises rare classes according to class frequency and complexity; and BLN preserves spatial coherence across modalities while reducing computational overhead. ECABEV reaches 39.96 IoU on nuScenes with a ViT-B/14 backbone at 224x224, improving on BEVCar in both accuracy and efficiency across diverse environmental conditions, including rain, night and motion-blurred scenes.
