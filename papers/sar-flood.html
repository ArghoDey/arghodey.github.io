Parallel Attention-Recurrence and State-Transition for Bi-Temporal SAR Flood Segmentation

Md Minhazul Islam, Yunfei Yin, Argho Dey, Sijing Xiong, Zumnan Timbi and Sourabh Choudhary

Information Sciences, under review.

Abstract

Bi-temporal SAR flood mapping is usually posed as static change estimation between two acquisitions. We present Parallel Attention-Recurrence and State-Transition, a transition-centric architecture that treats flood detection as temporal state-transition inference rather than static change estimation. Parallel attention-recurrence blocks model the recurrence of scene state across the pre-event and post-event acquisitions, and learned state transitions carry that state forward, so the segmentation of inundated areas follows from how the scene evolves rather than from a direct comparison of two images.
