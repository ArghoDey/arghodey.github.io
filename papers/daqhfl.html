Distribution-Aware Hybrid Classical-Quantum Federated Learning for Calibrated Long-Tail Medical Image Classification

Md. Rakibul Islam, Yunfei Yin, Md Imam Ahasan, Swachha Ray, Md Ariful Islam, Argho Dey and Xianjian Bao

World Wide Web, under review.

Abstract

Medical image classification under federated learning is difficult when the label distribution is long-tailed and skewed across clients, because a globally averaged model is poorly calibrated on the rare classes that matter clinically. We propose Distribution-Aware Hybrid Classical-Quantum Federated Learning (DAQHFL), a calibration-oriented framework that integrates client-local rarity weighting, hybrid representation learning and distribution-aware aggregation. Rarity weighting corrects the contribution of scarce classes within each client, the hybrid representation combines classical and quantum feature spaces, and aggregation accounts for the label distribution of each client rather than only its sample count.
