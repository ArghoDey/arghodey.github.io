A Dual-Stream Framework for Landslide Segmentation with Cross-Attention Enhancement and Gated Multimodal Fusion

Md Minhazul Islam, Yunfei Yin, Md Tanvir Islam, Zheng Yuan and Argho Dey

Computers, Materials & Continua, 2025.

PaperCode

Abstract

Automatic landslide segmentation from remote sensing imagery is challenging because traditional machine-learning and early CNN-based models often fail to generalise across heterogeneous landscapes, where landslide regions are sparse and fragmented. We propose a lightweight dual-stream Siamese framework that integrates optical and topographical data through cross-attention, gated fusion and sub-pixel upsampling in a single architecture. The decoder captures long-range context at deeper levels with lightweight cross-attention and refines spatial detail at shallower levels through attention-gated skip fusion, enabling precise boundary delineation with fewer false positives; gated fusion improves the integration of optical and topographical cues; deep supervision stabilises training and improves generalisation; and learnable sub-pixel upsampling replaces transposed convolution to mitigate checkerboard artefacts. Despite its compact design, the model consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines, reaching an F1-score of 0.9110 and an IoU of 0.8839 on the Bijie dataset, with comparable gains on Landslide4Sense.
