T-GeoBEV: Temporal Geometry-Aware Panoramic BEV Semantic Mapping

Argho Dey, Swachha Ray, Minghui Wang, Yihan Zhou, Xueyang Guo and Yilin Mo

ACCV 2026, under review.

Abstract

Bird's-Eye-View semantic mapping is crucial for autonomous driving, providing a comprehensive spatial representation of the scene. Methods that use a single panoramic image simplify the sensor setup, but they rely on implicit, learned view transformations that lack explicit 3D geometry and temporal reasoning, leading to spatial inaccuracies and instability in dynamic environments. We present T-GeoBEV, a framework for temporally consistent and geometry-aware panoramic BEV perception built from three components: a geometry-aware BEV projection module that replaces the implicit transformation with an explicit, depth-based lift-splat-project mechanism, ensuring an accurate spatial layout; a temporal BEV memory module that employs a ConvGRU to aggregate multi-frame BEV features, enabling robust understanding of dynamic scenes and occlusions; and a distortion-aware panoramic encoder that mitigates equirectangular projection artefacts by injecting spherical coordinate embeddings and employing deformable convolutions. Unifying explicit geometry, temporal context and distortion-aware processing, T-GeoBEV advances the state of the art in single-panorama BEV perception, with experiments on nuScenes-360 and DeepAccident-360.
