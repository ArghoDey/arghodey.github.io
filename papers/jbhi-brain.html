Reliability-Aware Subset-Consistent Multi-Scale Disentanglement for Incomplete Modality Brain Tumor Segmentation

Swachha Ray, Yunfei Yin, Argho Dey, Hongyu Liu, Zhiqiu Huang, Md Rakibul Islam, Zheng Yuan, Sijing Xiong, Zumnan Timbi and Md Minhazul Islam

IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, under review.

Abstract

Brain tumour segmentation from multimodal MRI is essential for clinical diagnosis and treatment planning, yet most existing methods rely on complete, high-quality multimodal imaging - an assumption often violated in practice through missing modalities, acquisition variability, noise and image degradation. We propose Reliability-Aware Subset-Consistent Multi-Scale Disentanglement, a reliability-aware and stability-driven framework for robust segmentation under incomplete-modality conditions. The framework separates modality-invariant anatomical information from modality-specific appearance features at multiple semantic levels. A reliability-aware gating module estimates the quality of anatomical features from each available modality and suppresses unreliable contributions before fusion, while subset-consistency regularisation encourages stable representations across different modality combinations of the same subject. On BraTS 2020, across all 15 possible missing-modality combinations, the method achieves Dice scores of 89.32%, 82.58% and 66.97% for whole tumour, tumour core and enhancing tumour, outperforming the state of the art by 1.78%, 2.95% and 1.97%, with consistent gains on BraTS 2015 and BraTS 2018.
