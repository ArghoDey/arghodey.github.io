RESAR-BEV: An Explainable Progressive Residual Autoregressive Approach for Camera-Radar Fusion in BEV Segmentation

Zhiwen Zeng, Yunfei Yin, Zheng Yuan, Argho Dey and Xianjian Bao

IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2026.

Paper

Abstract

Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) semantic segmentation provides comprehensive environmental perception for autonomous driving but suffers from multi-modal misalignment and sensor noise. We propose RESAR-BEV, a progressive refinement framework that advances beyond single-step end-to-end approaches: (1) inherently interpretable, coarse-to-fine refinement using a residual autoregressive learning paradigm, implemented by a Drive-Transformer and Modifier-Transformer cascade in which each stage is responsible for a specific semantic scale, from road topology to lane boundaries; (2) a robust BEV representation combining ground-proximity voxels with adaptive height offsets and dual-path voxel feature encoding (max and attention pooling); and (3) decoupled supervision with offline ground-truth decomposition and online joint optimisation, preventing overfitting while ensuring structural coherence. Experiments on nuScenes show that RESAR-BEV achieves state-of-the-art performance with 54.0% mIoU across seven driving-scene categories while maintaining real-time capability at 14.6 FPS, and remains robust in long-range perception and adverse weather.
