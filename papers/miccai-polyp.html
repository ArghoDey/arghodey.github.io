Robust Colonoscopy Video Polyp Segmentation via Quality-Aware Memory and Prototype Alignment

Sijing Xiong, Yunfei Yin, Argho Dey, Zheng Yuan, Zumnan Timbi, Swachha Ray and Hongyu Liu

MICCAI 2026.

Paper

Abstract

In colonoscopy video polyp segmentation, degradations such as motion blur, specular reflections and occlusions often render single-frame features incomplete and unreliable. Most existing methods nevertheless rely on current-frame features to construct queries for cross-frame retrieval and alignment, and their performance drops under such degradations. We propose a query-enhanced cascaded memory framework integrating Quality Awareness (QA) and Prototype Alignment (PA). A quality-aware sensory memory mechanism adaptively gates memory updates based on observation reliability and state consistency, constructing stable query representations for robust retrieval; and a prototype alignment mechanism maintains appearance prototypes as long-term anchors to mitigate appearance drift. By leveraging stable queries to retrieve relevant prototypes, the framework enables reliable cross-frame association. On the most challenging Unseen-Hard subset of SUN-SEG, our method improves the best baseline by 2.9% in Dice and 4.2% in sensitivity, demonstrating superior robustness under degraded conditions.
