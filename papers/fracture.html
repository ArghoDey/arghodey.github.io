An Iterative Latent Semi-Supervised Network for Fracture Risk Prediction

Zheng Yuan, Yunfei Yin, Fengning Chuan, Argho Dey and Swachha Ray

Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, under review.

Abstract

Accurate prediction of osteoporotic fracture risk is crucial for early intervention and personalised treatment, but existing methods require large-scale baseline data and long-term follow-up to obtain enough positive samples, while clinical tools such as bone mineral density and FRAX extract limited additional structure from DXA or X-ray images. To achieve accurate prediction under limited labelling while exploiting multimodal clinical data, we propose an Iterative Latent Fusion-based Heterogeneous Cross-Training Network (ILF-HCTN), a semi-supervised framework with two core ideas: a heterogeneous dual-model (Charger and Adapter) cross-training network that introduces persistent network perturbation to avoid homogenisation, and an Iterative Latent Fusion module that dynamically aligns DXA image features with structured clinical data, resolving the dimensional mismatch in multimodal fusion. On the MOF-Cohort (1198 patients) and the NSCLC-Radiomics dataset, ILF-HCTN outperforms five representative semi-supervised methods by 3.79 in mean absolute error and 20.84% in mean absolute percentage error, achieving reliable performance with less than 10% labelled data.
