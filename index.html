Biography

Argho Dey is an algorithm engineer at Beijing Lingyu Intelligent Technology (灵御智能), in the Product Architecture Department and Research Institute, where he builds evaluation frameworks for vision–inertial localization. He received his M.Sc. in Computer Technology from Chongqing University in 2026, ranking first among the international cohort, and his B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangxi Institute of Technology in 2023. He is an incoming PhD student in the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University.

His research is on making robot perception measurable rather than merely accurate: multi-sensor fusion, calibration and state estimation for machines that have to localize, map and avoid obstacles in real environments. His prior work is in Bird's-Eye-View perception for autonomous driving — camera–radar fusion, temporal reasoning and planning on a shared representation — and he has contributed 13 papers, 5 published and 8 under review, across perception, medical image analysis, remote sensing and federated learning.
