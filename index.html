About

I am an artificial intelligence and computer vision researcher with an M.Sc. in Computer Technology from the College of Computer Science at Chongqing University. I completed my master's degree in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Yunfei Yin, ranking first in my cohort with a CGPA of 3.76/4.00. I also ranked first in my undergraduate cohort while earning a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangxi University of Technology.

My primary research focuses on reliable perception and planning for autonomous driving, particularly Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) scene understanding, multimodal camera–radar fusion, temporal reasoning, panoramic BEV mapping, and efficient deep learning for resource-constrained environments. My master's thesis investigated spatial–temporal reliability optimization of BEV representations for perception and planning.

I also contribute to interdisciplinary research in medical image analysis and remote sensing, including incomplete-modality brain tumour segmentation, colonoscopy video polyp segmentation, fracture-risk prediction, SAR flood segmentation, and multimodal landslide mapping. My broader goal is to develop robust, explainable, and computationally efficient AI systems that operate reliably under uncertainty, incomplete information, and sensor noise.

Selected Research

  • ECABEV — A resource-efficient camera–radar BEV segmentation method using de-noise enhanced channel attention.
  • DTP-HMamba — A dynamic token-pruned hierarchical Mamba framework for incomplete-modality brain tumour segmentation.
